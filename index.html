The premise

Part portfolio, part laboratory, part field notebook.

Throughout my career I’ve been hired as a writer, operator, project manager, educator, consultant, and systems designer. Those jobs looked different on paper, but they all involved the same behavior: notice what’s being mistaken for normal, name it, and build something to address it.
