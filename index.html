What you get when you dig into this site

This site is part portfolio, part laboratory, and part field notebook. Over the years I've worked in operations, project management, technical writing, education, and consulting. Everything on this site started the same way...I noticed something people had accepted as normal.

Some pages document professional work and the environments that shaped my thinking. Others explore frameworks I've developed, including the Operational Entropy Index, or explain what it's like to work with me.

You'll also find teaching projects like Inglés Rebelde, experiments like the Playlist Project, technical writing, creative writing, prototypes, business ideas, and a variety of other side quests. Most began the same way: I encountered a system, assumption, or classification that didn't quite make sense to me, and started exploring alternatives.

Some of these projects are finished, some are active, and others are snapshots of my thinking in motion. I keep them together because the professional and playful work continually inform each other. The same instincts that shape an operations framework can also appear in a teaching method, a playlist taxonomy, a game, or a job-search postmortem.

If you're here for a quick overview, start with the TL;DR. If you want evidence of how I work, explore the experience pages and testimonials. If you're interested in the ideas and experiments themselves, follow whichever title makes you curious.

The experience pages serve as field notes from different stages of my career. The framework pages are the maps that emerged from those experiences, and everything else is what happens when my curiosity gets enough room to wander.

Feel free to explore in any order. The pages are more connected than they first appear.
